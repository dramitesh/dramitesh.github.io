01 / ACADEMIC PROFILE

Medicine, education & cardiometabolic health.

Dr Amitesh Aggarwal is Director Professor of Medicine at UCMS & GTB Hospital, Delhi. His career encompasses clinical medicine, postgraduate and undergraduate medical education, clinical research, health policy, institutional academic administration and national/international professional collaboration.

His stated research themes include premature and young-onset coronary artery disease, cardiometabolic risk, hypertension, diabetes, frailty in older adults and tobacco-related cardiovascular disease.
